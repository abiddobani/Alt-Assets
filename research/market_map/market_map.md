## How each asset class is measured

### Real Estate Equity

- **Size (2025):** MSCI professionally managed real estate (global USD 13.5tn) x 95% G20 share; Canada ~3.5% of global
- **Duration (Office, Industrial & logistics, Residential (multifamily), Retail, Other / alternatives):** WALE: weighted average remaining lease term (building economic life is 40-60y)
- **Duration (Hotels):** No leases: nightly revenue (operator contracts run 10-20y)
- **20-yr return:** NCREIF NPI property-type index (US, unlevered, gross of fees); NPI total = 6.3% (2006-2025; gross of fees)

### Infrastructure Equity

- **Size (end-2025):** Unlisted infra fund AUM (USD 1.6tn incl. 8.1% debt funds) x 93% G20; shares from 2025 deal volume by sector. Excludes listed infra and direct LP holdings
- **Duration:** Contracted / regulated cash-flow life at entry (asset life in notes)
- **20-yr return:** Estimate of net fund-level return; EDHECinfra/PGIM asset-level gross returns 2007-2022 run 3-5pts higher (~2006-2025; net of fees)

### Public Equity

- **Size (31 Dec 2025 (sector weights 30 Sep 2026)):** WFE global market cap USD 151.9tn x 93% G20 share, split by MSCI ACWI sector weights; Canada = TSX+TSXV CAD 6.4tn at 1.37
- **Duration:** Perpetual claim; shown as implied duration ~ forward P/E (sector P/Es scaled to ACWI 16.3x)
- **20-yr return:** MSCI ACWI sector indices in USD with dividends (net of withholding tax), estimate; ACWI total ~8.2% p.a.; verify on Bloomberg (2006-2025; gross of fees)

### Private Equity

- **Size (end-2024):** Preqin global PE AUM USD 8.6tn (incl. VC and dry powder) x 95% G20; strategy split estimated; Canada built from CVCA deal flow
- **Duration:** Typical holding period per investment (fund term 10-12y)
- **20-yr return:** Cambridge Associates US pooled net IRR (20-yr horizon) (20 years to Dec 2025; net of fees)

### Infrastructure Debt

- **Size (2025):** Estimate: project and infra-company loans and bonds outstanding (~USD 0.8-1.05tn a year of issuance x ~4.5y average life); excludes plain utility corporate bonds
- **Duration:** Weighted-average life of the loans or bonds (amortising)
- **20-yr return:** Estimate: no 20-yr infra debt index; IG ~ long BBB corporate returns plus an illiquidity premium (~2006-2025; gross of fees)

### Illiquid Term Loans

- **Size (end-2025):** PitchBook private debt AUM incl. perpetual vehicles (USD 2.56tn) less real estate debt (~0.25tn) and infra debt (~0.13tn) x 97% G20; strategy split estimated
- **Duration (Senior direct lending, Junior & mezzanine, Asset-based & specialty finance, Venture debt):** Weighted-average life (5-7y maturities, early repayment)
- **Duration (Distressed & special situations):** Typical holding period
- **20-yr return (Senior direct lending):** Estimate for senior-only; Cliffwater Direct Lending Index (all loans, gross, unlevered) 9.5% over 20y (2005-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Junior & mezzanine, Asset-based & specialty finance, Venture debt):** Estimate (gross) (2005-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Distressed & special situations):** Estimate (gross, to match the other private credit rows); net fund IRR roughly 9-10% (2005-2025; gross of fees)

### Private Liquid Loans

- **Size (end-2025 (Europe Oct 2025)):** Broadly syndicated leveraged loans: US USD 1.55tn + Europe EUR 338bn + ~USD 50bn other; rating split estimated. CLOs hold ~70% of US loans (not additive)
- **Duration:** Credit WAL (7y maturity, frequent refinancing); floating rate so rate duration ~0.25y
- **20-yr return (BB-rated and above):** Estimate for BB sub-index; Morningstar LSTA US LLI total 5.1% p.a. (gross) (2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Single-B):** Estimate for B sub-index; Morningstar LSTA US LLI total 5.1% p.a. (gross) (2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (CCC and below):** Estimate for CCC sub-index, net of default losses; above single-B to reflect the higher risk premium (2006-2025; gross of fees)

### Commercial Mortgages

- **Size (Q4 2025 (US); other countries estimated):** Bottom-up: US USD 4.99tn (MBA) + EU, UK, Japan, Australia, Korea, Canada, other G20 estimates; excludes China developer loans (~USD 1.9tn). Holder split: US from MBA, elsewhere ~80% banks
- **Duration:** Weighted-average life of the loans
- **20-yr return (Bank balance-sheet loans):** Estimate (no index); proxy Giliberto-Levy commercial mortgage index (~2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Agency & insured multifamily):** Estimate: proxy Bloomberg US Agency CMBS index (~2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Insurer & pension core mortgages):** Estimate: proxy Giliberto-Levy commercial mortgage index (~2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (CMBS & CRE CLO):** Estimate: proxy Bloomberg US CMBS (ERISA eligible) index (~2006-2025; gross of fees)
- **20-yr return (Debt funds & mortgage REITs):** Estimate (gross); bridge ~6-8%, mezzanine ~9-11% (~2006-2025; gross of fees)
